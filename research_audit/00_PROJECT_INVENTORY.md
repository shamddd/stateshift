## 3. Storage Hierarchy and Separation of Concerns

To guarantee research integrity and prevent silent overwriting of frozen historical evidence:
- **`stateshift_all_documents/`** serves as the immutable historical archive.
- **`research_audit/`** contains all analytical verifications, reconciliations, statistical reproductions, and NeurIPS policy audits.
- **`neurips_submission/`** contains the isolated, rebuilt, camera-ready NeurIPS submission candidate and checklist.

No historical files are modified or deleted during this audit.
