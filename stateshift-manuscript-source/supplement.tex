\section{Exact Discrete Binomial Power Modeling (Study 2)}
In paired binary trials, let $K \sim \text{Binomial}(N, q)$ denote the number of discordant pairs, where $q = p_{10} + p_{01}$. Conditional on $K$, the number of successes under Condition F follows $B \sim \text{Binomial}(K, \theta)$, where $\theta = p_{10} / q$. Under the null hypothesis $H_0: \theta = 0.50$, the exact two-sided rejection region at level $\alpha = 0.05$ is defined by critical values $c_L(K)$ and $c_U(K)$ such that $P_{H_0}(B \le c_L \text{ or } B \ge c_U) \le 0.05$.

The unconditional power of the exact McNemar test is the expectation over the distribution of $K$:
\begin{equation}
\text{Power}(N, q, \theta) = \sum_{k=1}^N \binom{N}{k} q^k (1-q)^{N-k} \left[ \sum_{b=0}^{c_L(k)} \binom{k}{b} \theta^b (1-\theta)^{k-b} + \sum_{b=c_U(k)}^k \binom{k}{b} \theta^b (1-\theta)^{k-b} \right]
\end{equation}
Under pilot parameters ($q = 0.10, \theta = 8/10 = 0.80$), unconditional power is $36.19\%$ at $N=100$. The minimum sample size achieving $\ge 80.0\%$ unconditional power is $N = 231$ paired problems ($1 - \beta = 80.02\%$).
