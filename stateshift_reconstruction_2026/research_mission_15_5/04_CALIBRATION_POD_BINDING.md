# 04 Calibration Pod Binding Protocol

**Module:** `research_mission_15_5/auth/session_authorizer_v2.py`  
**Tests:** `test_blocker_3_calibration_pod_mismatch_rejected`, `test_scenario_D_pod_mismatch_rejected`.

---

## 1. Mandatory Pod-ID Binding
In Mission 15.4, calibration runs did not require `pod_id` in the authorization. Mission 15.5 establishes that **no paid GPU execution can occur without exact pod binding**:
- When `mode="CALIBRATION"`, `pod_id` cannot be `None`, empty, or a wildcard.
- The authorization payload must match the CLI `--pod-id` argument byte-for-byte.
- If a pod fails or is replaced, a new signed authorization document must be generated by the PI for the new pod ID.

## 2. Cross-Mode Protection
- An authorization generated with `mode="CALIBRATION"` cannot be passed to a runner executing `--mode PRODUCTION`.
- Attempting cross-mode reuse raises `ValueError: Mode mismatch: document authorized CALIBRATION, runner requested PRODUCTION`.
