When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity: Pitfalls of Uncertainty-Weighted Credit Assignment in Language Model Reinforcement Learning
Sham Satish Thakare
Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence (IEEE TAI), 2026
In language model reinforcement learning (RLVR/GRPO), weighting policy gradients by outcome confidence proxies has been proposed to focus updates on uncertain reasoning steps. We expose completion-length confounding in token predictive entropy (r = +0.486, 95% CI [+0.318, +0.627]), which collapses to non-significance (r_partial = -0.092, p = 0.365) after controlling for sequence length. We demonstrate that sample-level uncertainty weighting yields zero online advantage (d = 0.00, 80.00% ± 0.00% Pass@1) over standard outcome-supervised GRPO across N=3 matched seeds, misidentifying correct complex derivations as uncertain in 42.1% of cases.

StateShift: State-Matched Evaluation of Error Recovery in Neural Reasoning
Sham Satish Thakare
Submitted to Elsevier Artificial Intelligence (AIJ, Manuscript ID: ARTINT-D-26-01491), 2026
Standard evaluations of reinforcement-learning post-training emphasize aggregate benchmark accuracy, masking behavioural shifts conditional on intermediate reasoning state. StateShift introduces a counterfactual difference-in-differences protocol matching Recovery (invalid step) and Control states. We observe an 11.76-percentage-point interaction contrast (Γ_256 = +0.1176, 95% bootstrap CI [+0.0955, +0.1400]) that is statistically detectable by checkpoint step t=32, robust across strict decontamination filtering.

Predicting Reinforcement-Learning Plasticity of Intermediate Language-Model Checkpoints: A Cross-Architecture Diagnostic Study
Sham Satish Thakare
Working Paper, 2026 (Target under consideration: JMLR)
Post-training policy gradient optimization across language model checkpoints is computationally expensive. We propose CARLS, a pre-hoc diagnostic probing framework analyzing representation rank and gradient spectral norms across intermediate checkpoints. Across Pythia and Qwen lineages, CARLS reliably identifies high-plasticity checkpoints prior to RL execution, saving 18.7% of total RL training FLOPs.

AdaptiveReplica: Dynamic Quorum Adaptation and Failure-Aware Replica Selection in Distributed Consensus
Sham Satish Thakare
Working Paper, 2026 (Target under consideration: IEEE TPDS)
Static quorum configurations in Raft consensus clusters suffer severe tail latency degradation during asymmetric network partitions. We propose a C++20 dynamic quorum adaptation algorithm that tracks moving-window peer latency distributions and re-weights voting subsets while maintaining strict linearizability and quorum intersection invariants, reducing p99 write latency by 41.3%.

TraceMind: Graph-Constrained Causal Reasoning for Root-Cause Localization in Microservice Systems
Sham Satish Thakare
Working Paper, 2026 (Target under consideration: IEEE TCC)
Microservice failure cascades generate floods of correlated telemetry alerts that mislead naive anomaly detectors. TraceMind projects OpenTelemetry distributed trace spans onto directed acyclic dependency graphs and applies topological causal constraints to filter downstream symptoms, achieving 88.4% Top-1 root-cause localization accuracy in under 350ms.
