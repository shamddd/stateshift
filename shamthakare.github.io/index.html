I am Sham Satish Thakare, an independent computer science researcher and Machine Learning / Systems Engineer based in Pune, India. Broadly, I am interested in building reliable, verifiable, and adaptive intelligent systems. My research investigates the intersection of foundation model reasoning dynamics—specifically Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)—runtime agent enforcement, and fault-tolerant, latency-resilient distributed systems.

I have been fortunate to receive systems research mentorship from Yuri Shelke (Distinguished Engineer at Capital One), collaborating on failure-domain aware latency profiling, dynamic quorum adaptation, and low-level C++20 concurrency protocols in distributed consensus (quorumshift / AdaptiveReplica).

In foundation model post-training and reasoning, my work investigates credit assignment bottlenecks. In my manuscript, When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity (under consideration at IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence), I examine how token-predictive entropy correlates strongly with sequence length ($r = +0.486$), systematically confounding uncertainty-weighted credit assignment in GRPO on multi-step reasoning derivations. Additionally, I formulated state-matched continuation evaluation protocols (recovery_eval, submitted to IEEE BigData 2026) to isolate error recovery resilience from generative capacity under frozen structural covariates.

In autonomous agent safety, I developed MediRush-SafeAgent—a layered runtime enforcement framework for healthcare-commerce LLM tool execution (available on SSRN, under review at Intelligence-Based Medicine).

Earlier, I completed my B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at APCOER Pune (CGPA: 8.70/10.00), where I conducted undergraduate thesis research advised by Prof. Sagar Gawande and Prof. Sunil Thakare, and served as a teaching assistant for undergraduate coursework in Data Structures, Algorithms, and Foundations of Machine Learning. Currently, I engineer high-throughput parallel data pipelines and cloud telemetry infrastructure at Spectra Corporate Service.
