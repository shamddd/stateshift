About Publications Research CV

I am Sham, an independent computer science researcher based in Pune, India. My research broadly focuses on foundation model reasoning, reinforcement learning (RLVR / credit assignment), and reliable intelligent systems.

I study the empirical and theoretical failure modes of multi-step reasoning, uncertainty estimation in post-training, and verifiable execution. Recently, my work has focused on exposing length-confounding artifacts in token predictive entropy for outcome-supervised reinforcement learning (GRPO), and establishing state-matched difference-in-differences evaluation protocols for neural error recovery.

Currently, I work as a Data Engineer / Machine Learning Specialist at Spectra Corporate Service, where I engineer high-throughput data processing systems and machine learning data pipelines.

I graduated with a B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (Cumulative GPA: 8.70 / 10.00) from APCOER Pune (Savitribai Phule Pune University). Previously, I was a research intern at APCOER Pune, investigating algorithmic problem solving and statistical machine learning evaluation.

When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity: Pitfalls of Uncertainty-Weighted Credit Assignment in Language Model Reinforcement Learning
Sham Satish Thakare
Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence (IEEE TAI), 2026
Falsifies token-predictive entropy as an uncertainty proxy in LM RLVR. Demonstrates predictive entropy correlates strongly with sequence length (r = +0.486, p < 0.001), collapsing under length controls, while uncertainty-weighted GRPO yields zero online gain over outcome supervision.

Sham Satish Thakare

Research Focus

Reasoning Reliability & RLVR

Investigating credit assignment in language model reinforcement learning, exposing heuristic proxy failures, and developing length-invariant credit estimators.

State-Matched Error Recovery

Designing difference-in-differences counterfactual evaluation protocols to measure whether LLMs recover from erroneous reasoning steps.

Representation Plasticity (CARLS)

Analyzing gradient spectral properties and feature rank across pre-training checkpoints to predict downstream RL reward plasticity.

Distributed Systems & Consensus

Developing dynamic quorum adaptation and failure-domain aware replica selection in C++20 Raft consensus clusters under network partitions.
