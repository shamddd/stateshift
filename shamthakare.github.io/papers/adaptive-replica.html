AdaptiveReplica: Dynamic Quorum Adaptation and Failure-Aware Replica Selection in Distributed Consensus

Sham Satish Thakare, Yuri Shelke

Under Review at IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence (IEEE TAI, Manuscript ID: TAI-2026-Aug-A-01875)

Abstract

In state-machine replication protocols such as Raft, static quorum commitments suffer from tail-latency degradation under asymmetric network faults, straggling peers, and non-uniform round-trip delays. In this paper, we formulate AdaptiveReplica—a failure-domain aware dynamic quorum adaptation mechanism implemented in C++20. By integrating online latency profiling and predictive replica selection, AdaptiveReplica continuously re-weights consensus quorums without violating linearizability invariants or safety thresholds, eliminating tail latency spikes under asymmetric fault injections.
