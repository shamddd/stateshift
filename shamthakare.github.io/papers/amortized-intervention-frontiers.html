Amortized Intervention Frontiers for Language-Model Reasoning: When Does Training Beat Search?

Sham Satish Thakare

Abstract

Formalizes Deployment-Amortized Intervention Frontiers trading off training compute C_train against inference search volume Q. Proves that compositional out-of-distribution (OOD) length extrapolation systematically accelerates break-even crossover (R_f ≈ 0.0618 ≪ 1.0), making up-front RLVR cost-effective in <100 queries on OOD tasks vs >1,200 queries on IID tasks.
